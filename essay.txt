The person I hold in the highest regard is my grandmother, whose selflessness, perseverance and responsibility have left an indelible impression on me. What I admire most is the patience and responsibility with which she supports our family whenever difficulties arise.

I still remember a time when I performed poorly at school and wanted to give up. Instead of criticizing me, she spared no effort to encourage me and helped me regain confidence. She taught me to view difficulties as stepping stones to progress. Her actions speak louder than words. More importantly, I learned that perseverance matters most when circumstances are unfavorable and that true strength lies in patience and responsibility.

Her example has exerted a profound influence on me far beyond that experience. Whenever I encounter setbacks, I follow her example, keep my composure and move forward rather than avoid problems. Because of her, I have become more resilient, responsible and considerate. To me, she is not only the person I respect most, but also a constant source of strength.
