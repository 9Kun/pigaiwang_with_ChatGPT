The person I hold in the highest regard is my grandmother, whose character has left a profound and indelible impression on me. Marked by patience, perseverance and responsibility, she has cared for our family with quiet devotion. Specifically, I admire these qualities because they sustain our family whenever difficulties arise.

To illustrate this, I vividly remember receiving a disappointing grade and wanting to give up. Nevertheless, she offered no criticism; instead, she spared no effort to encourage me, thereby restoring my confidence. Furthermore, it was she who taught me to view difficulties as stepping stones to progress. More significantly, I learned this from her; perseverance matters most when life becomes difficult, and true strength is shown through patience and responsibility.

That lesson has profoundly shaped how I address difficulties. Whenever I face setbacks in my studies, I follow her example, maintain my composure and persevere. Therefore, under her influence, I have become more resilient, responsible and considerate. For these reasons, she is the person I respect most, someone I admire and look up to, and a constant source of strength.
