The person I hold in the highest regard is my grandmother, whose character has left a profound and indelible impression on me. Marked by patience, unwavering perseverance and a pronounced sense of responsibility, she has always cared for our family with quiet devotion. Specifically, I admire her patience and responsibility whenever difficulties arise.

To illustrate this, I vividly remember receiving a disappointing grade and wanting to give up. Nevertheless, instead of criticizing me, she spared no effort to encourage me and restore my confidence. Furthermore, she taught me to view difficulties as stepping stones to progress. More significantly, I learned this from her: perseverance matters most when life becomes difficult, and true strength is shown through patience and responsibility.

Finally, that lesson has profoundly shaped how I address difficulties. Subsequently, when I encounter setbacks in my studies, I follow her example, maintain my composure and face challenges with perseverance. Therefore, I have gradually become more resilient, responsible and considerate. For these reasons, she is not only the person I respect most, but also a constant source of strength.
