The person I hold in the highest regard is my grandmother, whose character has left an indelible impression on me. Marked by patience, unwavering perseverance and an exceptional sense of responsibility, she has cared for our family with quiet devotion. Specifically, I admire the patience and responsibility she displays whenever difficulties arise.

To illustrate this, I remember receiving a disappointing grade and wanting to give up. Nevertheless, instead of criticizing me, she spared no effort to encourage me, thereby restoring my confidence. Furthermore, she taught me to view difficulties as stepping stones to progress. More significantly, I learned from her: perseverance matters most when life becomes difficult, and true strength is shown through patience and responsibility.

Finally, her influence has profoundly shaped how I address difficulties. Subsequently, whenever I encounter setbacks in my studies, I follow her example, maintain my composure and face challenges with patience and perseverance. Therefore, I have gradually become more resilient, responsible and considerate. For these reasons, she is not only the person I respect most, but also someone I will always look up to.
