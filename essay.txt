The person I hold in the highest regard is my grandmother, whose character has left an indelible impression on me. Marked by patience, perseverance and responsibility, she cares for our family with devotion. Above all, I admire the patience and responsibility she displays in supporting our family whenever difficulties arise.

First, I remember receiving a disappointing grade at school and wanting to give up. However, instead of criticizing me, she spared no effort to encourage me and restore my confidence. Second, she taught me to view difficulties as stepping stones to progress. Third, I learned from her that perseverance matters most in adversity and that true strength requires patience and responsibility.

Finally, that lesson, rooted in her perseverance and responsibility, has influenced how I address difficulties. Thereafter, whenever I encounter setbacks, I follow her example, maintain my composure and face challenges with patience and perseverance. Therefore, I have gradually become more resilient, responsible and considerate. For these reasons, she is not only the person I respect most, but also someone I look up to and a constant source of strength.
